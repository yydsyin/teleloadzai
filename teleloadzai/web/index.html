    Your log-ins and cookie file
    What is a cookie?
    A small piece of text a site saves when you log in, which says "this is me". The log-in window takes only the cookies of the one site you log in to, and gives them only to the download program.
    Two ways to add a log-in
    Log in on the site Recommended
    Import a cookie file A cookies.txt from your browser
    How to get a cookies.txt file
    Add a cookie-export add-on to your browser (search its add-on store for "cookies.txt"), and save your cookies with it. If it asks which kind of file, choose Netscape. Your file is never changed.
    Teleloadzai keeps only the sites it can download from and throws the rest away before saving. Cookies from your bank or your mail are never kept.
