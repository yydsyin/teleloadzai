    What is a cookie, exactly?
    A small piece of text a site saves when you log in, which says "this is me". The log-in window takes only the cookies of the one site you log in to, and they go straight to the download tool and nowhere else.
    Two ways to add a log-in
    Log in on the site Recommended
    or
    Import a cookie file A cookies.txt from your browser
    How to get a cookies.txt file
    Add a cookie-export add-on to your browser (search its add-on store for "cookies.txt"), and save your cookies with it. If it asks which kind of file, choose Netscape. Your file is never changed.
    Teleloadzai keeps only the sites it can download from and throws the rest away before saving — your bank and your mail never come in.
    Downloading a lot while logged in can get that account suspended. Use a second account.
