Accounts

Use my log-ins for downloads off
Some links only work when you are logged in: a private Instagram post, an age-restricted YouTube video, a Kuaishou video. Log in to that site below, and Teleloadzai keeps its proof of log-in — a cookie — for the download tools. You never type a password into Teleloadzai, and the cookies stay on this PC.
What is a cookie, exactly?
A small piece of text a site saves when you log in, which says "this is me". The log-in window takes only the cookies of the one site you log in to, and they go straight to the download tool and nowhere else.
Downloading a lot while logged in can get that account suspended. If you do this often, use a second account.
